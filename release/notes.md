### Added

- **Images:** Revision notes in Compare images are kept with each image until your assistant's results answer them, so you can note several images and send them together with **Copy revision task** in the footer; Compare shows each image's earlier notes, applies and restores under its notes.

### Fixed

- **Images:** Copy revision task no longer refuses an image already in the game with "Every image in your list is already in the game."; the revised image can be applied again.
- **Images:** the copied revision task now spells out your Revision notes, so your assistant can't miss them.
- **Images:** Mark user reviewed no longer files your Revision notes as the review's reason, and is no longer offered on an image already in the game.
- **Images:** when the translation skipped every image left in your list, Copy translation task now says so instead of "Tick the images to translate first." or "Every image in your list is already in the game."
